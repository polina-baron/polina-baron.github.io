**Abstract.** We study the Kontsevich--Zorich cocycle, its fixed tensor constructions, and their finite direct sums over products of strata of Abelian differentials. The acting groups \(G\subset(\mathrm{SL}_2\mathbb{R})^n\) are, up to factor permutations and conjugation by coordinatewise rotations, products of diagonal \(\mathrm{SL}_2\mathbb{R}\) blocks and a finite central group. For ergodic \(G\)-invariant probability measures, we establish Deligne-type semisimplicity: the bundles split into Hodge-compatible summands \(V_i\otimes_{A_i}W_i\), where \(A_i\) are division algebras, \(V_i\) carry pure variations of Hodge structure, and \(W_i\) carry finite direct sums of pure Hodge structures. Invariant subbundles correspond to \(A_i\)-submodules of the multiplicity spaces \(W_i\).

If the marginal measures of the diagonal blocks have smooth local supports and are locally equivalent to smooth volume, Hodge-orthogonal projectors onto invariant subbundles are locally polynomial in period coordinates on the normalized support. The proof combines a local product-of-quadrics model with polynomiality along stable and unstable leaves. On the cone formed by scaling each diagonal block together, projection coefficients admit homogeneous degree-zero expressions involving integral powers of reciprocal block areas.

For the full product group \((\mathrm{SL}_2\mathbb{R})^n\), single-stratum measure classification provides the support hypothesis, giving rigidity for every ergodic invariant probability measure. Under the corresponding transported hypothesis, arbitrary coordinatewise conjugates also have polynomial equivariant projectors, although these need not be Hodge-orthogonal. Genus-two examples show that nonrotational conjugation can break Deligne-torus invariance and that scaling factors independently may require square roots of area functions.
